### 4. Macro backdrop (what's changed)
- **Inflation:** The debate has flipped from "when does the Fed cut" to "the Fed is hiking into an oil shock." August CPI/PPI both ran hot, and now a geopolitically-driven energy spike is layering additional cost-push pressure on top of an already-hawkish setup just one day before the decision.
- **Growth/labor:** No new labor data flagged in today's research; the broader narrative remains that the labor market has stayed resilient enough to keep a hike on the table rather than force the Fed toward easing.
- **Geopolitics:** The dominant cross-asset driver remains the US-Iran conflict, now well into its stretch of escalation — Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline (~4mbd capacity) remains shut after drone strikes, Iran-Gulf diplomatic talks were postponed, and the Houthis reportedly control Perim Island in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, a chokepoint for Red Sea shipping. Analysts are flagging a realistic path to $120/bbl oil if disruptions widen.
- **Policy/AI:** A new and fast-moving cross-current: Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's essay calling for the industry to "pace the frontier" — deliberately slow the rate of frontier-model capability gains to allow more time for safety/alignment work — was publicly endorsed by both Sam Altman (OpenAI) and Elon Musk, a rare show of unity among rival AI labs. Markets read this as bearish for the aggressive AI-capex trade; OpenAI is reported to be delaying its IPO as a result. This is actively repricing chip/AI-infrastructure names (NVDA, AVGO, MU, INTC, LRCX all sold off hard Monday) and is the single biggest micro story layered on top of the Fed/oil macro backdrop.
- **Micro / sector rotation:** Defensive healthcare (LLY, MRK, ABBV, JNJ) and select software/services names outperformed Monday's broad tech selloff; financials were mixed-to-weak (BAC guided down on IB fees; GS, C, JPM, WFC all lower) while insurance/asset-management names were comparatively quiet. Energy is the clear sector-level beneficiary of the oil spike. Watch VIX (17.82, +4.2%) for further follow-through into the Fed decision.
