### Macro backdrop (what's changed)
- **Inflation/Fed:** Warsh's hawkish tone is the single biggest narrative shift of the past week, moving the market from pricing eventual cuts to pricing meaningful odds of a hike. Not all economists are convinced a hike actually lands, so expect volatility around every data point (today's ISM Services, Friday's jobs report) as the market handicaps the Sept 16 decision.
