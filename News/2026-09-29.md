### 4. Macro backdrop (what's changed)
- **Inflation:** Core PCE (due Wed) is the week's key inflation print, forecast to tick up to ~3.4% YoY — a hotter-than-expected number would reinforce the hawkish Fed pricing described above and could extend the bond selloff/yield spike.
- **Growth/labor:** JOLTS and consumer confidence today; ADP and GDP (third estimate) Wednesday; ISM Manufacturing Thursday; the September jobs report Friday is the week's capstone, with consensus near 85K.
- **Geopolitics:** The dominant driver right now. Iran offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and resume nuclear talks in exchange for the US lifting its naval blockade of Iranian ports, waiving oil-sanctions, and observing a ceasefire including Lebanon; Trump rejected the offer, saying Iran was "losing so badly." That rejection reignited Hormuz supply-disruption risk, sending oil and yields sharply higher Monday and pressuring equities. Watch for any escalation or de-escalation headlines intraday — this is the single biggest swing factor for both stocks and rates this week.
- **Micro / sector rotation:** Defensives (staples like PG, WMT; parts of healthcare like MRK, JNJ, ABBV, PFE) outperformed Monday while semis/tech were mixed-to-weak (QCOM, INTC, NOW, SNPS down; PANW, NVDA, AMAT up on idiosyncratic news) and industrials were hit hard by Boeing's FAA setback. Financials broadly softened (BAC, GS, MS, BLK all down 1-2%) even as higher long yields would normally help net-interest-margin stories — a sign the risk-off/oil shock narrative dominated sector-specific dynamics Monday.
