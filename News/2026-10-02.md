Fed Governor Michael Barr has been the most vocal hawk, repeatedly arguing inflation is "not clearly trending toward target in a timely way." Today's and next week's data will meaningfully move these odds.

Oil's large year-over-year gains continue to carry a geopolitical risk premium tied to Venezuela and Middle East supply dynamics, a theme that energy-earnings previews (XOM, CVX) are flagging into Q3 reporting season.

Consumer-facing names are more idiosyncratic: Nike's China/turnaround troubles stand out against resilient discretionary spending elsewhere.
