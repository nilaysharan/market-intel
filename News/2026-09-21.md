### 4. Macro backdrop (what's changed)
- **Inflation/growth/labor:** The hawkish Sept 16 hike reflects the Fed's view that inflation has been running too hot for too long; August factory data was soft (US manufacturing output -0.3%), a mild stagflationary cross-current the market is trying to price alongside higher-for-longer rates.
- **Geopolitics:** The 2026 Iran war remains the top tail risk - Trump has warned of severe consequences (economic collapse / leadership change) if Tehran doesn't reach a deal, while Iran continues to restrict the Strait of Hormuz and has targeted US-linked shipping/forces; Iranian President Pezeshkian is at the UN General Assembly in New York this week, keeping a diplomatic track alive alongside the military one. Brent is still expected to trade in a wide $90-110 band through year-end given this overhang. The Trump-Xi summit Thursday is the other major swing factor - low-to-moderate expectations per some commentary, but a broad trade-truce extension or de-escalation would be a risk-on catalyst; failure to progress (or a Taiwan flare-up) would be a risk-off one.
- **Micro/rotation:** Chip/AI-linked names (NVDA, AMZN, GOOGL, MRVL) and Bitcoin/Ethereum are leading pre-market strength, extending the "growth/AI risk-on" bias despite mega-cap dispersion (MSFT notably weak last week, -2.2% Friday). European banks were the standout laggard Friday (-2.25%) as yields rose. VIX is low (~15-16), signaling calm, but that calm sits on top of a hawkish Fed and an active shooting-war risk premium in oil - a combination that can unwind quickly on a single headline.
