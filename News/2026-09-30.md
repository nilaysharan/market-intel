### Macro backdrop (what's changed)
- **Inflation:** Core PCE has now held at 3.3% YoY for two straight months with no clear path back to the Fed's 2% target in sight — today's release (due after this report's generation time) is the key swing factor for the session. Sticky inflation alongside a hiking Fed is an unusual, stagflation-adjacent setup that's weighing on sentiment.
- **Growth/labor:** ISM Manufacturing (Thu) and nonfarm payrolls (Fri) are the week's other headline releases; early consensus points to a soft ~50K payroll gain with unemployment steady at 4.1%.
- **Geopolitics:** The Iran war (into its seventh month) remains the dominant oil/inflation risk factor — US forces have engaged IRGC-linked tankers near the Strait of Hormuz, but Gulf producers, backed by the US Navy, have pushed oil/product flows back to ~80% of pre-war levels, and September's Middle East export recovery is helping ease the acute supply-disruption premium that drove oil above $100 earlier in the month. Any escalation/de-escalation headline remains a same-day tape-mover. Separately, a government-shutdown scare was resolved weeks ago (CR through Dec 11), removing a near-term fiscal risk.
- **Micro / sector rotation:** Persistent inflation and elevated yields have hurt Republican approval numbers and raised the political stakes of the Nov midterms (control of Congress). In markets, rate-sensitive sectors (banks, REITs, smaller-cap names reliant on financing) are described as under the most pressure from the higher-for-longer yield backdrop, while AI-capex-linked tech/semis (ahead of Micron's earnings) remain a focal point for positioning into today's close.
