- **Inflation:** July PCE data (released late Aug) came in hotter than expected and unchanged from June — headline PCE 3.7% YoY, core PCE 3.3% YoY (vs. 2% target), core monthly pace accelerating to 0.2% from 0.1%. Separately, Qualcomm's CEO is joining a US business delegation for a Trump-Xi summit trip to China, with semiconductor export rules and market access on the agenda — a swing factor for chip-sector sentiment (AMD's thesis in particular is flagged by analysts as sensitive to any tightening of China export rules).
